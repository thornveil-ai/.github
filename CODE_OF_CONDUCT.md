# Code of Conduct

Thornveil maintains a professional, focused engineering environment across all of our repositories, discussions, and communication channels. This document sets the baseline.

## Standards

We expect everyone participating in Thornveil projects to:

- **Be professional.** Treat others with respect. Disagree on technical substance, not on people.
- **Stay on topic.** Issues, discussions, and pull requests are for engineering work, not for unrelated debate.
- **Communicate clearly.** Write what you mean. Provide context, reproduction steps, and references.
- **Assume competence.** Most disagreements come from missing context, not bad intent.
- **Respect operational sensitivity.** Many Thornveil users operate in regulated or classified environments. Do not pressure anyone to share details about their deployment, data, or workflows.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following will result in moderation, removal from threads, or being banned from the organization at our discretion:

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language
- Doxxing, threats, or attempts to identify another contributor's affiliation against their wishes
- Sustained off-topic or low-quality posting
- Spam, advertising, or commercial solicitation unrelated to the project
- Sharing proprietary, classified, or export-controlled information through public channels
- Deliberately disruptive behavior intended to derail a project or discussion

## Reporting

Report violations privately to **conduct@thornveil.ai**. Reports will be reviewed by Thornveil maintainers and handled confidentially. We do not require reporters to confront the offending party.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all official Thornveil spaces — GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other communication channels operated by Thornveil. It also applies when an individual is officially representing Thornveil or one of its projects in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may, at their discretion:

- Issue a private warning
- Edit or remove comments, commits, code, or other contributions
- Temporarily or permanently ban a contributor from one or more repositories or the entire organization

Decisions made by Thornveil maintainers are final.
