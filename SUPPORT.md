# Getting Support

Thornveil maintains several channels depending on what you need. Pick the right one and you'll get a faster answer.

## I Found a Bug

Open an issue on the relevant product repository using the **Bug Report** template. Include:

- The product and version (`rigrun --version`, etc.)
- Your operating system and hardware
- Steps to reproduce
- What you expected vs. what happened
- Logs or stack traces

## I Have a Feature Request

Open an issue using the **Feature Request** template. Lead with the problem you're trying to solve, not the solution you have in mind — we may have a better path.

## I Have a Question About Usage

For general questions, open a Discussion on the relevant product repository. The community and Thornveil engineers monitor Discussions and will respond as time allows.

If you have a question that touches sensitive details about your deployment, email **jesse@thornveil.ai** directly.

## I Need Commercial Support

Thornveil offers paid support, deployment services, and custom-agent engineering. This includes:

- Production deployment assistance
- Custom agent development through Agent Factory
- Air-gapped or classified-environment integration
- Long-term support contracts and SLAs
- Federal contracting (SBIR, OTAs, IDIQs)

Reach out to **jesse@thornveil.ai** with a brief description of your environment and what you need.

## I Found a Security Issue

**Do not file public issues for security problems.** Follow the process in [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md). Report privately to **security@thornveil.ai**.

## Response Times

| Channel | Typical first response |
|---|---|
| Security reports (`security@thornveil.ai`) | Within 72 hours |
| Commercial inquiries (`jesse@thornveil.ai`) | Within 1 business day |
| Public issues and discussions | Best-effort, usually within a week |

Community support is provided as time allows. Paid support contracts include defined SLAs.
